Course Examinations
========================================

This section contains comprehensive information about all course examinations, including schedules, procedures, and preparation materials. **The Winter Session STAT 35000-001 section is fully online and asynchronous; both exams are proctored online via Respondus LockDown Browser with webcam monitoring.**

General Exam Policies
------------------------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Exam Day Essentials
   :class: important

   For both exams:

   - **Exams are administered online in Brightspace using Respondus LockDown Browser** with webcam and audio monitoring.
   - Each exam is open for a **24-hour window** — the Midterm on day 6 (December 31) and the Final on day 11 (January 9). Once you begin, you have a fixed time limit (80 minutes for the Midterm; 140 minutes for the Final).
   - **One attempt only** — the exam cannot be paused, exited, and restarted.
   - **Government-issued photo ID** required for identity verification before the exam starts.
   - **No breaks** during the exam. Leaving the camera view will be flagged.
   - Exams are **closed book but open note** — your own handwritten or printed notes only. No tablets, iPads, phones, or other electronics.
   - **No formulas are provided.** Statistical table URLs are allowed by the LockDown Browser, but printing a physical copy is recommended.
   - **Complete the practice quiz in Brightspace** before the Midterm to verify your setup.
   - Because of the condensed calendar, there are **no make-up exams**.

See :ref:`syllabus-winter-index` for the full exam policy, equipment requirements, and grading weights.
